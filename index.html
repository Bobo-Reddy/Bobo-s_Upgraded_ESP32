Altitude

The SEN66 uses this to correct its CO₂ reading for air pressure. Nothing else it measures uses it. Ground height from a terrain map is accurate enough: 10 m out changes the correction by about 0.1%.

CO₂ self-calibration

With self-calibration (ASC) on, the SEN66 treats the lowest CO₂ it sees over about a week as fresh outdoor air (around 400 ppm) and shifts its baseline to match. Leave it on for nodes that regularly get outdoor air. Turn it off for nodes that never do, like a closed room or a sealed enclosure, because there it drags readings down over time.

 

LTE band lock

Notes: read these before locking

Normally the modem picks the band and cell itself. A lock limits it to the bands you tick here.

  • A lock helps when one band at a site is bad and another is good. Run a band survey first: its results show which bands register and pass data at that spot.
  • A lock doesn't help when every band is bad, for example from interference across the spectrum, a shielded spot or an operator outage.
  • If a locked band disappears, the node has no service on it. The node takes the lock off by itself after two failed attaches, or after three cycles without an upload, and then stays on automatic until you press Apply again.
  • The modem keeps the setting in its own memory. The firmware puts the modem's own default back on Restore automatic, on an automatic revert, and on a full erase.
  • LTE only stops the modem falling back to 3G or 2G when LTE gets weak.
  • At a site where uploads keep failing, the node may not reach its record until it reboots, so power-cycle it after sending a change.
  • The operator list for each band shows who holds spectrum there, not where each band is switched on. The survey shows what's actually live at a site.
 

Band survey

The node tries each band on its own for up to a minute, notes the signal and whether data gets through, then goes back to its own band setting. It takes up to about 20 minutes and holds up one reading while it runs. Results land in the node's diagnostics file in the repo.
