Send over the node's WiFi

Any phone, including iPhone. Do the three steps in this order and stay on this page throughout. This is the node's own setup network, and it works the same whether the node ends up on WiFi or on LTE.

  1. Fill in everything above, including the connection choice, the QR scan and Auto capture GPS. All three work here, and none of them work on the node's own page.
  2. Open Settings → Wi-Fi and join SACAQM-Setup-XXXX with the password sacaqm-setup. Dismiss the sign-in sheet if one pops up, and come back here.
  3. Tap the button. The node's page opens with everything already filled in — check it and press Save and connect.

Status & logs

Waiting for a node.

The node is working when the setup network disappears, ESP32-Provision stops advertising, and the LED flashes green for five seconds every five minutes. A steady red LED means the last upload was refused. On LTE, give the first join up to two minutes before reading anything into the light — a cold cellular attach is slow.
